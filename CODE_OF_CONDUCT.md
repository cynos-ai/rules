# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors and maintainers are expected to communicate respectfully, discuss evidence rather than personal motives, welcome good-faith questions, and make participation free from harassment or discrimination.

Unacceptable behavior includes personal attacks, threats, sexualized attention, sustained disruption, publishing private information, or conduct that would reasonably make another participant feel unsafe.

## Evidence-focused disagreement

Cynos Rules expects strong technical disagreement. Challenge a proposed rule, experiment, interpretation, or design directly and precisely. Do not treat benchmark outcomes as judgments about a contributor's worth or intent.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainers or a GitHub Security Advisory when no other private channel is available. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close contributions, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action.

Maintainers handling a report should protect privacy, avoid conflicts of interest, and explain enforcement decisions to the affected parties when appropriate.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone is publicly representing the project.
